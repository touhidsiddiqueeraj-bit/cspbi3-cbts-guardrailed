\vspace{1em}
\noindent\textbf{Abstract.} Stacked idealisations inflate SCAPS-1D CsPbI3 projections. On one fully worked CsPbI3/CBTS stack, joint optimisation of thickness, doping, defects, gap, and both transport-layer affinities gives a permissive-box ceiling of \textbf{24.95\%}. Sampling a frozen routine-quality prior volume (96-point Latin hypercube over eight factors; ranges and measured anchors in Table~1 of the manuscript) gives a median of \textbf{18.0\%} with a 5--95\% prior-predictive envelope of \textbf{15.6--20.0\%}. This is a sensitivity envelope under stated priors, not a confidence interval on real devices. The 2024--2026 experimental records fall above that envelope. The ceiling record comparison is density-of-states conditional: with capped density of states, literature radiative recombination ($C_r=10^{-10}$~cm$^3$/s) lowers the ceiling to 21.86\%, below the 22.02\% certified record; with effective-mass physical density of states, the same radiative term gives 23.46\% ($N_v=4.1\times10^{18}$~cm$^{-3}$) and 23.04\% ($N_v=8.9\times10^{18}$~cm$^{-3}$), above it. Interface sensitivity is regime dependent, bulk defects dominate at routine defect density, and series resistance up to 5~$\Omega$cm$^2$ closes roughly half of the six-point fill-factor gap against measured devices, with the remainder attributed to nonradiative interface recombination (ideality above unity).
