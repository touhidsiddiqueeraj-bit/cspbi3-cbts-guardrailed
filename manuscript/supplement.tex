\section*{S2. Script verification and unit traps}

SCAPS definition files store SI units while script commands use practical units. A same-value check is therefore required before interpreting any scripted defect, doping, mobility, or radiative change. Setting absorber doping to $10^{15}$~cm$^{-3}$ reproduces the file baseline, while an SI-valued input injects a million-fold overdose and creates ghost cells.

A second trap concerns interface cross-sections: the script key for interface capture cross-section restructured the interface defect block in this campaign rather than scaling the addressed rate. The definition-file route reproduced the expected $N\times10$ result, while the script-key route did not. The working rule is to verify every defect-population change with a same-field query in the same script and to treat a wrong-direction efficiency move as a failed application.

A near-zero radiative test at $10^{-16}$ leaves the baseline unchanged to 0.0001 points. That establishes omission as below solver sensitivity for this comparison; no unavailable external equation is cited for the built-in radiative estimate.

\section*{S3. Extended screening detail}

The full Round~A and Round~B leaderboards, joint-affinity extensions, gap steps, doping--affinity checks, and contact-regime variants are audit detail. Their headline-relevant conclusions are retained in the main paper: the landscape is converged rather than climbing; the joint-affinity peak is interior rather than edge-sitting; gap steps track the activation-energy landscape as well as absorption; and contact tolerance collapses as affinities move to aggressive values.

The no-interface audit points are reported as disqualified by lack of radiative margin. The $N\sigma$ identities are retained as verification checks: symmetric interface scaling and bulk scaling reproduce the corresponding density-scaled runs, confirming that the tested rate scales with the intended product.

\section*{S4. Optical and current diagnostics}

The QE integral exceeds the converged IV current by 1.19~mA~cm$^{-2}$: 22.39 versus 21.20. The IV value reproduces across IV step sizes, while its internal generation/recombination integral gives 21.21. The QE curve is also identical under the tested illumination modes. Ideal step absorption gives 24.4 at 1.65~eV for the archived spectrum. After the stated 10\% front reflection, the corresponding ceiling is 21.96, below the QE integral. The IV current is therefore retained.

Spectrum file substitution through the script interface returned the default-spectrum result digit-identically, so independent spectrum discrimination was blocked rather than passed. The residual QE-path over-delivery is carried as a two-sided $\pm1.2$~mA/cm$^2$ absolute-scale systematic. A separate blue-response uncertainty of order 1~mA/cm$^2$ arises from absent dispersive parasitic absorption. These are stated as uncertainties, not corrections.

\section*{S5. Prior-volume accounting}

The headline envelope uses only the 95 converged in-prior runs: median 17.98\%, p5 15.55\%, p95 19.96\%, rounded to 18.0\% and 15.6--20.0\%. The eight $C_r=0$ runs have median 17.93\% and are reported separately. One edge-contact configuration diverged repeatedly and is recorded as non-convergent, not excluded as a crash.

The density-of-states extension gives median 18.18\%, p5 15.76\%, and p95 20.03\%, rounded to 18.2\% and 15.8--20.0\%. Its density-of-states rank importance is near zero in-volume, even though physical density of states swings the clean corner by more than a point. Corner sensitivity and volume sensitivity are therefore different statements.

Bulk and interface quality were sampled independently. Binning by the nominal interface-to-bulk velocity ratio does not reveal a clean internal crossing at this sample size. The ratio organises the two anchor regimes but does not resolve the ranking bin by bin.

\section*{S6. Calibration limits}

The measured-grade ladder climbs monotonically and saturates at 20.32--20.66\%, below the certified record. Published device tables do not provide enough defect-level detail to reconstruct portable bulk lifetime and interface velocity for a specific device. The ladder is therefore a transfer-error bound, not a device-specific hindcast. No posterior calibration is claimed because no device data enter the prior loop. The vapour/spin forward check (260~nm films, $N_t$ from reported lifetimes: 17.96\% vapour at $N_t=10^{13}$, 16.76\% spin at $N_t=5\times10^{14}$) recovers the correct order and plausible scale without fitting. The 22.02\% certified value (Dai et al.) is taken as the highest certified CsPbI3 value among the cited records (next certified: 21.43\% inverted, 20.35\% carbon HTL-free); the 22.16\% and 22.15\% laboratory values are carried for completeness but excluded from the certified ranking. The Xu et al.\ 22.15\% entry remains bibliographically unverified and is flagged as such in the bibliography.
